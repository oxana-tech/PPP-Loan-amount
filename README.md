# Small Business Loans in Ohio

Tools: Excel (data), Tableau (visualization and dashboard).

Project Goal:
Visualize the distribution of government support Identify priority business categories Assess the effectiveness of the PPP program in Ohio Ensure transparency in the use of budget funds. 

Project Description:
 Analysis of PPP (Paycheck Protection Program) loan distribution for small businesses in the state of Ohio, USA. The program is aimed at supporting small businesses during an economic crisis. What is being studied:
Distribution by business size (number of employees): From 1-4 to 250-500 employees Largest category: 20-49 employees (583,052 employees) Distribution by loan amount: $350K-$1M — largest category (21.1% = $6B) $150K-$350K — 15.2% ($4B) $50K and Under — 15.1% ($4B) $2M-$5M — 14.7% ($4B) Geographic analysis: Cities and addresses of borrowers Rural/Urban indicator Low Income Community indicator.

Conclusions and Insights:
1. Main Recipients:
Medium-sized loans ($350K-$1M) prevail — this indicates support for medium-sized businesses
The largest number of employees is covered in the 20-49 employee category
2. Fund Allocation:
$6 billion (21.1%) went to loans of $350K-$1M
This shows that the program is focused on supporting stable medium-sized businesses
3. Social Impact:
The presence of Rural/Urban and Low Income Community indicators allows for analysis of equitable distribution
It is possible to track whether vulnerable communities receive assistance
4. Lender Activity:
976 lenders issued nearly 300,000 loans
On average ~305 loans per lender.
